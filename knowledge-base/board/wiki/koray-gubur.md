## Core ideas
- **Topical authority.** You earn rankings by covering a topic *comprehensively and
  consistently*, not by chasing isolated keywords. Authority is a function of
  processing the whole topic with quality and historical consistency.
- **Topical map.** Plan the whole subject as a map of a **core section** (money /
  central entities) and **outer sections** (supporting context) before writing —
  one post ≠ one keyword; the network is the unit.
- **Semantic SEO.** Optimise for **entities and their relationships**, not keyword
  density. Distinguish **micro-semantics** (sentence/phrasing precision) from
  **macro-semantics** (how the whole content network relates).
- **Contextual coverage & source context.** A site's identity/context shapes what it
  can rank for; stay consistent with your context and cover it fully.
- **Rigor over hype.** Runs SEO A/B tests to reverse-engineer engine behaviour;
  openly skeptical of shallow "AI tool" hype — tools help, they don't replace the
  methodology.
