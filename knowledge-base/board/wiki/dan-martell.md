## Core ideas
- **Buy Back Principle.** Don't hire to grow; hire to *buy back your time*. Audit how
  you spend hours, offload the low-value/low-energy ones first.
- **Time audit + DRIP.** Sort tasks by value/energy; move work down the ladder of
  Delegation → Replacement → Investment → Production.
- **Systems & playbooks.** A business that depends on the founder is fragile;
  document repeatable playbooks so outcomes (not tasks) can be delegated.
- **AI as leverage.** Treat AI/automation as the newest, highest-leverage way to buy
  back time and scale output.
- **Freedom framework.** Wealth + freedom come from building the machine, then
  working *on* it, not *in* it.
