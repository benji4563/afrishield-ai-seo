## Core ideas
- **Homepage blueprint.** A service-business homepage must instantly answer *what you
  do, who it's for, and what to do next* — clarity above the fold, one dominant CTA,
  a logical scroll that handles objections and builds trust.
- **Clarity over cleverness.** Message-first design; if a visitor is confused, they
  leave. Copy does the heavy lifting; design serves the message.
- **Conversion path.** Deliberate hierarchy of CTAs, social proof, and trust signals
  down the page — the site's job is to convert, not to win design awards.
- **Local SEO fundamentals.** Google Business Profile, reviews, and local relevance
  as the practical levers for service businesses.
- **Content-as-funnel.** Uses a single channel (YouTube) as a client-acquisition
  funnel — a model of focused, compounding content.
