# AfriShield Voice

**The test:** does it sound like *a smart practitioner showing you something they discovered* — or like *an AI writing tool trying to sound like a marketer*? If the second, rewrite.

## Traits

| Trait | Means | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligent | Explains mechanisms, not buzzwords | "AI doesn't rank pages. It assembles an answer from sources it can read and trust." |
| Direct | The point in the first sentence | "Your reviews are real. AI just can't read them." |
| Practical | One thing the viewer can do or check | "Open yoursite.com/robots.txt. Look for GPTBot." |
| Curious | Starts from a question we actually had | "I wanted to know if AI knows us. It doesn't. Yet." |
| Human | First person, contractions, admits limits | "We got this wrong in August." |
| Confident | No hedging on what we know; clear labels on what we don't | "This worked. This one we can't prove yet." |
| Slightly playful | One dry line, never a routine | "Google knew the business existed. AI needed an introduction." |
| Business-focused | Money, time, customers — not technology for its own sake | "That missed call was a $400 booking." (only with a real or clearly hypothetical number) |

## Register by audience

- **Tourism & hospitality, SMB owners:** full warmth, local references, one joke allowed.
- **Professional services, corporate buyers:** one notch drier. Wry, never silly. No slang.
- **LinkedIn:** analytical and professional storytelling; humor as understatement only.
- **Threads:** conversational, first-thought energy, but still true.

## Mechanics

- Sentences under ~18 words on screen and in VO. One idea per sentence.
- Spoken pace 130–145 wpm. Write for the ear: say it aloud before approving.
- Numbers as numerals. Currency with the symbol. Hedge estimates ("roughly", "about").
- Name the viewer: "hotel owner", "safari operator", "accountant in Nairobi" — never "businesses" in general.
- English by default; French or Pidgin phrases only when the founder actually delivers them.

## Banned phrases and moves

"In today's digital world" · "Did you know…" · "Have you ever wondered…" · "game-changer" · "revolutionize" · "unlock" · "leverage" · "supercharge" · "skyrocket" · "10x" · "secret hack" · "guaranteed" · "AI is changing everything" · "If you ask me…" as an opener · rhetorical triple-questions · emoji walls · fake urgency ("before it's too late") · empty motivation · generic "5 AI tools" lists without a real reason.

## Before / after

| Generic (reject) | AfriShield |
|---|---|
| "Our AI SEO service helps businesses improve visibility." | "I asked ChatGPT for the best boutique hotels in Arusha. Here's who it named — and why." |
| "Voice agents are revolutionizing customer service!" | "Nobody leaves voicemails. So what happens to the 9pm call?" |
| "5 tips to rank on AI search." | "One page on your site decides whether AI can quote you. Let's check yours." |
| "We helped a client grow 300%!" | (Not allowed unless the story bank holds the evidence and permission.) |
