## Never

- Invent clients, results, revenue, conversations, reactions, screenshots, testimonials or statistics.
- Claim "first", "#1", "guaranteed" or any ranking outcome.
- Name a real business negatively. Punch at the industry, at dead tactics, or at ourselves.
- Use Meta/Google/OpenAI logos as if endorsed; show real product UI only as screen-recorded proof.
- Use a cartoon or AI-generated character as the primary on-camera presence (TikTok standing rule).
- Sell in the hook. The product is the answer at the end of a story.

## CTA doctrine

Never "buy now". Never the same CTA two days in a row. Stage matches the month's funnel (awareness → consideration → conversion). Comment keywords (`AI`, `GEO`, `VISIBLE`, `AUDIT`, `BUILD`…) are the lead-capture device on TikTok/IG/FB; LinkedIn and Threads use a question or a reply prompt instead (see `platform-guide.md`).
