## Voice, tone, style rules

- AfriShield voice: light, human, slightly cheeky. One or two smiles per script, not stand-up.
- African audience first. FCFA-native, USD only for foreign viewers.
- Neighborhood references OK: Akwa, Bonapriso, Bonamoussadi, Dragage.
- Numbers must feel real — round and hedged ("roughly", "an estimated") on modeled figures.
- Character delivery: confident, not preachy. On-camera carries hook + pivot + CTA. Voice-over carries numbers and audit walk-through over b-roll.
